Dream Cat Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

Dream Cat is a video game developed and published by Sunny Labs. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Dream Cat.

1. Information Stored by Dream Cat

Dream Cat does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, or other contact information to play.

Gameplay information, such as scores, achievements, unlocks, and in-game currency, is stored locally on your device. Sunny Labs does not operate a cloud-save service or receive this gameplay information through its own servers.

2. Advertising and Third-Party Data

Dream Cat offers optional rewarded advertisements through Google AdMob. You may choose whether to watch an advertisement to receive an in-game reward.

When advertising services are used, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP addresses, app interactions, advertising interactions, and diagnostic or technical information. This information may be used for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and, where permitted, advertising personalization.

Google's handling of information is described in its policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

Sunny Labs does not receive your personal advertising identifiers directly for its own independent use.

3. Advertising Choices and Consent

Watching rewarded advertisements is optional. Dream Cat's core gameplay does not require you to watch advertisements.

Where required by applicable law, advertising-related consent and privacy choices will be presented through the app. You may also be able to manage advertising preferences through your device settings and Google's available privacy controls.

4. Sharing of Information

Sunny Labs does not sell player account information or locally stored game progress. Advertising-related information may be processed by Google and its advertising partners as described above and in their respective privacy policies.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay progress is stored on your device and is not maintained in a Sunny Labs cloud account. Removing the app or clearing its app data may delete locally stored progress, depending on your device's backup and storage behavior.

Information processed by Google through AdMob is subject to Google's applicable data retention and deletion practices. For privacy questions, contact Sunny Labs using the address below.

6. Children's Privacy

Dream Cat is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed toward children under 13. The game does not require players to submit personal contact information to Sunny Labs.

Advertising and related data processing are subject to applicable children's privacy requirements and platform policies. If you are a parent or guardian with a privacy concern, please contact us.

7. Security

Sunny Labs does not operate player account databases or cloud-save servers for Dream Cat. We take reasonable care in selecting and implementing third-party services, but no method of electronic data processing is completely secure.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Dream Cat evolves, including if we introduce new features or services. The updated policy will be published at this address with a revised effective date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request consent before new data practices take effect.
